# Warm-up adherence — measured 25 Sep 2026 (S15)

Question: does the athlete do the ramps the card prescribes, and does he add ramps the card leaves out? Measured on the export (`tests/fixtures/export/`, sets June–23 Sep; stored plans exist from 1 Sep, so the prescribed-vs-logged join covers September only).

## Logged warm-ups, all lift-sessions since June (485)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Lift-sessions with any logged warm-up | 223 (46%) |
| Warm-up sets when present | median 2, mean 1.8 |
| Warm-up load as a fraction of the top set | median 0.64 (p25 0.56, p75 0.79) |
| Warm-up rows carrying a target from the card | 390 |

By lift: the first compound of a session and the heavy compounds are ramped (Pull-Ups 100%, Machine Chest Press 100%, Leg Press 95%, Machine Shoulder Press 95%, Lat Pulldown 68%, T-Bar Row 68%, Cable Row 64%); isolation lifts almost never (Tricep Pushdown, Hammer Curl, Cable Lateral Raise, Cable Chest Fly 0%; Face Pulls, Ab Crunch Machine 5%). That is the programme's own rule (ramp the first movement and a new loading; none where the muscle is warm) being followed, not ignored.

## Reading

Adherence is high and the deviations are sensible: a ramp added on a heavy row the card judged warm, never a ramp skipped on a first movement. Nothing to build. Two things to watch, not fix:

1. The "no ramp — muscle already warm" rule is overridden by the athlete on the second and third pulls about a third of the time. If that persists into October it is a candidate for the rule to say "one light set optional" rather than "no ramp" on rows over ~90 kg.
2. The join only sees September. Re-run at the block review with the wider window.
